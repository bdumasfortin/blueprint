Launch decision

How should Fieldnote become a weekly habit?

Fieldnote turns scattered client signals into one concise Friday briefing for an 18-person consultancy. The launch shape must earn attention without creating another destination people are expected to remember.

Working recommendation Ship the digest with a lightweight archive.

Use email to establish reach and a small web archive to preserve retrieval, sharing, and future structure.

Proposed pilot

Build evidence in three releases.

The archive stays deliberately small until the team demonstrates retrieval behavior. No personalized feed, alerts, or client workspace is included in the pilot.

Establish the signal

Publish a consistent digest that is short enough to finish and specific enough to use.

  • Five signals
  • One owner per signal
  • One Monday-use prompt

Preserve each issue

Create a stable web page for every digest without adding a separate navigation habit.

  • Permanent URL
  • Client and topic tags
  • Copyable excerpt links

Observe retrieval

Add structure only after the team searches, revisits, or shares past signals in real work.

  • Weekly usefulness check
  • Retrieval examples
  • Decision on deeper workspace
What could change the decision

Four assumptions carry the recommendation.

These are not implementation risks. They are claims about the team’s behavior that the pilot needs to expose.

A1

Email remains a trusted attention channel.

If newsletters are routinely ignored, the recommendation overstates the reach advantage.

A2

Five signals can be edited into a coherent brief.

If the content needs deep context, the digest may become a teaser rather than the product.

A3

Monday usefulness can be observed credibly.

The current success signal is behavioral but has no defined collection method.

A4

A lightweight archive stays lightweight.

Search, permissions, taxonomy, and analytics pressure could turn the archive into the dashboard by accident.

Decision requested

Approve digest + archive for the pilot.

Preserve the dashboard as a later option only if observed retrieval makes a dedicated workspace materially more useful.

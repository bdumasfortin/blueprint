How should Fieldnote become a weekly habit?

What could change the decision

Four assumptions carry it.

These are claims about team behavior for the pilot to expose, not implementation risks.

A1 · ATTENTION

Email remains trusted.

If newsletters are routinely ignored, the recommendation overstates the reach advantage.

A2 · EDITING

Five signals form a coherent brief.

If the content needs deep context, the digest may become a teaser rather than the product.

A3 · EVIDENCE

Monday usefulness can be observed.

The current success signal is behavioral but still needs a collection method.

A4 · SCOPE

The archive stays lightweight.

Search, permissions, taxonomy, and analytics could turn it into the dashboard by accident.

Decision requested

Approve digest + archive for the pilot.

Keep the dashboard as a later option only if observed retrieval justifies a dedicated workspace.

Evidence on demand

Two assumptions need testing.

The recommendation is strong enough for a prototype, not strong enough to skip observing operators with a full queue.

A1 / Detail width

A 13-inch laptop may leave the detail pane too narrow for logs and ownership controls. The prototype should test 1280-pixel and 1440-pixel widths before the split ratio settles.

A2 / Queue persistence

Keeping the queue visible may distract operators during high-severity work. Test whether a temporary focus treatment is enough before considering a separate incident route.

Decision requested

Prototype the queue + detail pane.

Preserve a one-column fallback for narrow screens and test the split at realistic queue volume.

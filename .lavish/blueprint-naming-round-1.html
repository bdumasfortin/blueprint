Name study / round 01

A name for the pause before it ships.

Blueprint is useful as a project codename, but it points toward planning and architecture. The product we have actually designed is a quiet visual instrument where a person inspects an agent’s work, gathers private feedback, and decides when it moves.

01
Working recommendation: advance Lenticle.

It best captures “focused seeing” without trapping the product in documents, code review, planning, or generic AI language. Pactile is the strongest behavioral alternative.

Preliminary screen only
The naming contract

What the name must carry

One word, comfortable in speech and at a terminal, broad enough for visual reviews beyond software, and calm enough to respect human control.

Show, don’t govern

A viewing or editorial instrument—not an autonomous agent, control plane, approval bureaucracy, or project planner.

Deliberate, not live

The reviewer works privately, queues one considered packet, and reveals staged revisions when ready.

Crafted, not cute

Distinctive and memorable without fantasy spelling, AI suffixes, overused “flow / forge / hub,” or a name that needs a sales pitch.

A focused visual instrument for human–agent decisions.

A lenticle is one lens in a larger optical surface. The metaphor fits a tool that makes one decision legible at a time while the artifact remains central.

Watch: the word is unfamiliar and slightly technical. It will need one clean pronunciation cue at launch—but it has a precise visual identity and room to grow.

Collision evidence

Why this shortlist is unusual

Dozens of stronger-looking obvious names were eliminated because the adjacent category is saturated with 2025–2026 agent, review, and local-first products.

Examples: Margent is already a local-first agent review app; Aldine already combines anchored review threads and AI fixes; Winnow already combines human and AI feedback; and Cesura already oversees coding agents. “Blueprint” itself also collides with existing AI product methodology and established software brands.
